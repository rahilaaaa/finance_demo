## Source-defined uncertainties

- Customer Products and Vendor Products are named navigation destinations, but the specification does not fully define product-master behavior. Their screens focus on invoice/bill line references and expose that scope boundary.
- PAN Entities is named in the accounting navigation, but the specification says its exact field-level behavior is not fully exposed. Business rules must be confirmed before finalizing its data fields.
- Statutory rates, thresholds, tax filing eligibility, bank provider support and payment gateway behavior require approved policy and verified integrations.

## Prototype boundary

The project contains illustrative records and simulated UI interactions. It does not persist to a backend or perform real journal posting, statutory calculations/filings, bank synchronization, payment capture, OCR, server-side authorization or immutable audit persistence.
